## [1.0.0] — 2025-03-04

### Added
- Initial release of the `pro-financial-model-india` skill
- 25-sheet institutional-grade Excel model for NSE/BSE-listed companies
- Full Ind AS compliance (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow)
- India-specific data gathering workflow (Screener.in, BSE/NSE filings, Google Finance CMP)
- SOTP valuation sheet for conglomerates and holding companies
- SEBI BRSR ESG framework (Sheet 22)
- Promoter pledge risk flagging (>40% threshold)
- Indian tax rate: 25.168% (base 22% + surcharge + cess); MAT; Section 115BAB
- MCLR-linked debt tracking and unhedged forex debt flag
- Companies Act Schedule II depreciation rates
- Ind AS 116 ROU Asset schedule (leases)
- F&O data on Technical Analysis sheet (OI, PCR, futures basis)
- SEBI circuit breaker levels (5%/10%/20%)
- CRISIL/ICRA/CARE credit rating grid
- 11 sector-specific KPI sets (BFSI, NBFC, IT, Pharma, Cement, Auto, FMCG, Real Estate, Power, Metals, Telecom)
- Indian macro assumptions (G-Sec 10Y, India ERP, RBI repo rate, CPI, GDP)
- CCI M&A review threshold (₹2,000 Cr) and SEBI Open Offer / SAST rules
- SEBI Buyback Regulations 2018 compliance (max 25% cap)
- Related party transaction (RPT) flagging (>10% of revenue)
- Corporate action adjustment guidance (QIPs, Rights, Bonus, Splits)
- 5-tier rating system (BUY / ACCUMULATE / HOLD / REDUCE / SELL)
- Quality checks checklist (balance sheet, CFS ties, EPS adjustments, ROCE/ROE)
